# Research chain and evidence

The central progression is **reproduce → diagnose → train for handoff states → integrate → retest**. The sequence below describes decisions supported by data, not a claim that every attempted configuration succeeded. Links target the complete local repository records.

[Baseline report](../../locomotion_reproduction/README.md). | Retain local 1499; attribute robot/task/rewards/PPO settings to reference. Do not claim strict full-quality reproduction. |

## Interpretation

The central research contribution is the explicit connection between **interfaces, skill sequencing, state-dependent handoff, watchdog timing and quantitative evaluation**. The evidence also shows why more training, more action blending or slower braking cannot be assumed to improve the whole integrated system.

The reference project supplied the locomotion foundation;local work reproduced training and developed the diagnostic/skill-integration experiments. This preparation project is not a claim of a novel general humanoid architecture or completed hardware safety.

## Retained evidence

Recent experiments preserve state/observation/action/proposal traces, stage/watchdog events, configurations, weight/model/source hashes and independent verification outputs. Full local raw storage is Git-ignored;compact copies here are summaries, not the full training dataset. Some early baseline metrics were transcribed from terminal output and lack complete immutable training-time provenance. Current hashes cannot retroactively repair those omissions. Per-node protocols differ;do not sum all case counts into one independent statistical sample.
